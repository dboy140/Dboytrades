# ICT vs NBBTRADER — where they diverge

## The honest headline

**The comparison is lopsided and this section is weaker than intended.**

Of 59 extracted rules, **3** are NBB-sourced (`SB-010`, `MM-010`, `SMC-001`).
That is not an editorial judgement about his material — it reflects what exists:

- his own channel holds **16 videos**, several of them beginner-level series
  ("Trading For Beginners: ...")
- the substantive long-form content is guest appearances, and after quarantining
  misattributed hits only a handful survived
- five candidates were removed entirely as not being him (see Gate 2 notes)

So this is a diff between a very large ICT corpus and a small NBB one. Treat
every claim below as provisional.

## The pattern, as far as it goes

Where both cover a concept, **NBB is more mechanical and ICT is more
contextual**. `SMC-001` is the clearest case: NBB gives an if-this-then-that
definition of a market structure shift — sweep the lows, take the high that
preceded them, look left for targets — where ICT's treatment explains why the
term matters but leaves identification to judgement.

`MM-010` follows the same shape: not new theory, but the observable that makes
existing theory actionable.

That matches the brief's expectation that his value is compression for
execution. **But it rests on three rules.** It is a hypothesis worth holding
lightly, not a finding.

## What would settle it

His full mentorship material is not in this corpus. The reuploads that surfaced
(`sult4n` Lessons 1 and 3, the "Trading For Beginners" series) are fragments.
If the diff matters, the gap to fill is a complete NBB mentorship sequence —
which likely is not on YouTube at all.
